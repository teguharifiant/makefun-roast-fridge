# Strategy

The roast avoids the two stock jokes the field is crowded with: the fridge
that orders too much milk, and the fridge that snitches to your doctor (seven
entries in the field already play the doctor angle, and the cry-into-the-ice-
cream setup is taken word for word by another entry). Instead it reframes one
specific, real behaviour — the ice dispenser with its lever, its lights and
its daily ritual — as a slot machine. The setup is the machine; the sting is
the payout, "crushed dreams", a pun on the crushed ice it actually dispenses.
One sentence, one line, 112 characters, no throat clearing.
